# AutoRide

**Automatic bike trip detection.** AutoRide recognises when you start cycling and records the
ride, without you touching your phone.

This site hosts AutoRide's legal documents. It exists so that the App Store and Google Play have
a stable public URL for them.

## Documents

- **[Privacy Policy](legal/privacy-policy.md)** — what the app stores, what leaves your device,
  and how to delete everything
- **[Terms of Use](legal/terms-of-service.md)** — licence, safety, and the limits of what the
  app's measurements mean

## The short version

Your trips — including the GPS route of every ride — are stored only in the app's private storage
on your phone. There is no AutoRide account, no AutoRide server, and no analytics or advertising
SDK in the app.

The one exception: viewing a map requests map images from the OpenStreetMap Foundation's servers,
which necessarily reveals your IP address and the area of the map you are looking at to that
third party. If you never open a map, the app makes no network requests at all. This is explained
in full in [§3 of the Privacy Policy](legal/privacy-policy.md).

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
