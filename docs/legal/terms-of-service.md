## 4. Accuracy — what the app does not guarantee

AutoRide estimates. It does not measure with certified precision.

- **Trip detection can be wrong.** It can miss a ride, invent one, record a car journey or a
  train ride as cycling, or start and stop at the wrong moment. Detection uses motion patterns
  and GPS speed against thresholds; it is probabilistic by design and reports a confidence score
  precisely because it is not certain.
- **GPS is imprecise.** Distance, speed, altitude and route geometry are subject to GPS error,
  signal loss in tunnels and cities, and the accuracy your device chooses to provide. Battery
  optimisation deliberately reduces GPS sampling, which reduces precision further.
- **Battery behaviour varies.** The app aims for low drain, but actual consumption depends on
  your device, its age, signal conditions, and the operating system's own decisions.
- **The operating system can stop the app.** iOS and Android may suspend or terminate background
  activity at any time, especially under battery pressure. Background trips can be missed for
  reasons entirely outside the app's control. iOS in particular grants background execution
  opportunistically, so automatic detection is less reliable there than on Android.

**Do not rely on AutoRide** as the sole record for anything that matters: insurance claims,
legal proceedings, competitive results, medical or training decisions, expense or mileage
reimbursement, or any official reporting. If a ride matters, verify it independently.

## 5. Your data is your responsibility

Trip data lives only on your device. There is no server-side copy and no backup service operated
by the developer.

That means: **if you lose your device, reset it, uninstall the app, or tap "Clear all trips",
your riding history is gone and cannot be recovered by anyone, including the developer.**

On Android the trip database is deliberately excluded from platform backups for privacy reasons
(see the Privacy Policy §7.3), which means it is also excluded from platform *restores*. Keep
your own records of anything you would be upset to lose.
